When creating drafts for the new website, consider that the brand is not full established so we have creative freedom to experiment with these over the different designs 

The primary truck used by this company is light blue, about this color - #2fa8eb 

Diggers and other helping tools generally follow a gold and charcoal color scheme - #c4822d

Red and white can be seen as safety tape

Play with the design but consider the industry, it is not corporate, clear easy to read fonts will be more effective. This is also true for any copy that you generate that is not lorem ipsum, keep it simple and use English (UK) English spelling for this website.  

I want to use this first draft to prompt the business owner for additional information so include additional sections 'About the owner' 'Our Work' with a gallery, 'Our Customers' (testimonials), 'Get a quote' call to action, 'Our Work' / Skills - Just to suggest a few.
